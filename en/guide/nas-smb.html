Back up automatically to a NAS or Windows share

Save your Android photos and documents automatically to a NAS or Windows share (SMB) over your home Wi-Fi. No cloud required.

What you need

  • An Android device with ShareOrBackup installed (Android 8.0 or later)
  • A NAS, or a Windows PC with an SMB share
  • Your phone and the destination on the same Wi-Fi network

Setup steps

  1. Enable sharing on the destination
    On a NAS, enable SMB (Windows sharing). On a Windows PC, create a shared folder and allow file sharing.
  2. In the app, choose Add device → SMB
  3. Enter the connection details
    Address (IP or host name), port (usually 445), share name, username and password. If guest access is disabled, a username and password are required.
  4. Pick folders
    Choose the folder on your device and the shared folder to store the backup.
  5. Turn on automatic backup
    From then on, files are saved automatically whenever you are on the same Wi-Fi.
If it will not connect
  • Guest access is often disabled → set a username and password
  • The port is usually 445. Add custom ports manually
  • On Windows, allow "File and printer sharing" in the firewall

Backing up to a USB drive

Even without a NAS or PC, you can connect a USB drive or external storage to your device and pick it as the destination.

Connecting with SFTP

If your NAS or server offers SSH (SFTP), choose SFTP instead in the same flow. Traffic is encrypted.
