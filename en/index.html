FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is my data sent to the cloud?

No. Transfers stay inside your home network and are never sent to an external cloud.

Does sync continue if the app stops?

Yes. It runs in the background with the screen off and resumes automatically after a Wi-Fi reconnect or a scheduled revival.

Can I recover deleted files?

Versions are moved to .trash before delete or overwrite, and can be restored from history for 7 days.

Is there a monthly cloud fee?

No. It is free to use, and Pro is a one-time purchase.

Which destinations are supported?

App-to-app (P2P) plus SMB / SFTP / FTP & FTPS / WebDAV / S3-compatible.

What do I need to use a NAS?

Enable SMB (Windows sharing) or SFTP on your NAS. If it is on the same Wi-Fi, the app discovers it automatically and you just pick it from the list. No extra software is needed.

Can I use just a USB drive?

Yes. Connect a USB drive or external storage to your device and pick it as the destination. Automatic backup works even without a NAS or PC.

Can I use it outside my home?

It is designed for use on the same network. To reach storage at home from outside, you need a VPN or a similar setup.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 (API 26) and later are supported.

What is the difference between sync and backup?

Sync keeps two folders identical in both directions. Backup is a one-way copy from your device to the destination, protecting the original.

How is this different from Syncthing?

Both sync folders in two directions and avoid the cloud. The difference is scope and peers: Syncthing syncs devices that run Syncthing, over LAN or the internet. ShareOrBackup works mainly inside your home Wi-Fi and can also connect directly to existing servers such as SMB, SFTP, WebDAV and S3-compatible storage. Use Syncthing if you need to sync while away from home; use ShareOrBackup to move photos to your own NAS or PC.
